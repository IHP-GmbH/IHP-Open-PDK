Tech Info
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Technology   : SG13G2
Library      : sg13g2_stdcell_hv  (thick-oxide, 3.3 V standard cells)
Derived from : sg13g2_stdcell (Copyright 2023 IHP PDK Authors, Apache-2.0)
Maintainer   : ChipDesign B.V.  <info@chipdesign.be>
Date         : 18 Aug 2026

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 84 cells of sg13g2_stdcell rebuilt on the 3.3 V thick-oxide devices
(sg13_hv_nmos / sg13_hv_pmos): same topology, same pin names, same pin
order, cells named sg13g2_hv_* so both libraries coexist in one netlist.
The transform: gate length 130 nm -> 450 nm (Gat.a3 minimum), PMOS width
x 2.40 (re-centres the switching threshold at Vm/VDD = 0.5046, matching
the thin-oxide sizing intent), NMOS unchanged, supply 1.2 V -> 3.3 V.
Cost, measured on inv_1: input capacitance 2.20x, FO4 delay 2.66x.

Layout is a 1-D monotone retarget of the hand-drawn thin-oxide GDS on a
17-track (7.14 um) row with the CoreSiteHV 0.48 um site.

Important Notes:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. View coverage (work/view_matrix.py prints the full per-cell matrix):
==========================================================================================
- 84 cells in SPICE, CDL, Verilog, xschem symbol + schematic views.
- 84 cells in GDS and LEF (66 retargeted, 2 tie cells and 16 formerly
  skipped cells built by per-cell generators); all 84 are DRC clean
  (cell rules; the density rules are chip-level checks) under both the
  PDK's KLayout decks AND Magic's strict analog N-well rules, and LVS
  clean against the CDL.
- All 84 cells in the Liberty file, 74 of them with timing arcs:
  66 characterized with CharLib (combinational cells, 9 flip-flops with
  clk->Q / setup / hold, 5 latches with en->Q and closing-edge
  constraints) + 6 tri-state cells measured directly (data arc plus
  three_state_enable / three_state_disable) + sighold (driver_type
  bus_hold, measured capacitance and leakage, no timing arcs) + 2 tie
  cells (no arcs; measured leakage) + 7 area-only stubs for the physical
  cells (fill_*, decap_*, antennanp; measured leakage, measured diode pin
  capacitance). Every liberty cell has a layout; nothing is stripped at
  install time.
- The 2 statetable clock gates (lgcp_1, slgcp_1) carry the full ICG
  model: statetable, internal state pin, state_function on GCLK, the
  clock_gate_* pin attributes, CLK->GCLK propagation, setup/hold of the
  enables against the rising edge, minimum clock high and low time, and
  per-state leakage. CharLib has no input form for a statetable, so they
  are measured directly by work/char_clockgate/.

2. Liberty:
==========================================================================================
- One corner: typical, 3.30 V, 25 C (sg13g2_stdcell_hv_typ_3p30V_25C.lib;
  the library name carries the corner suffix, matching upstream).
- Per-pin max_capacitance / max_transition derived from the characterized
  table axes (load-axis top per output pin, slew-axis top per pin), plus
  default_max_capacitance 0.66 / default_max_transition 6.66968. Without
  them STA limit checks pass vacuously and OpenROAD TritonCTS crashes.
- NLDM, thresholds 20/80/50, 7x7 tables; grids are the thin-oxide grids
  scaled by the measured 2.66x delay / 2.20x capacitance ratios.
- Characterized with CharLib (charge-integration input capacitance) plus
  project sequential procedures; cross-checked against an independent
  characterizer (lctime) at 3132 table points: median 2.9 % on delays,
  0.0 % on transitions in the STA-relevant region.
- area is the measured LEF footprint for every cell (all 84 are drawn;
  no pre-layout estimates remain).
- Validated by OpenSTA read-back; the characterization method comparison
  and the library derivation are documented in doc/ (two PDF reports).

3. Verilog:
==========================================================================================
- sg13g2_stdcell_hv.v deliberately ships no copy of the shared ihp_* UDP
  primitives; use the PDK's sg13g2_udp.v alongside it, exactly as for the
  thin-oxide library. Loading two copies would collide.

4. LibreLane:
==========================================================================================
- libs.tech/librelane/sg13g2_stdcell_hv/ is a complete SCL: config.tcl
  (PLACE_SITE CoreSiteHV 0.48 x 7.14, HV driving/tie/fill/decap/diode/CTS
  cells), tracks.info, latch and mux techmaps, exclude lists, and
  sdfbbp_map.v.
- The PDK-level libs.tech/librelane/config.tcl gains a conditional block
  for STD_CELL_LIBRARY == sg13g2_stdcell_hv (LIB corner *_typ_3p30V_25C,
  VDD_PIN_VOLTAGE 3.30); libs.ref/sg13g2_stdcell_hv/lef/ carries a copy
  of the shared sg13g2_tech.lef because the PDK config globs it per-SCL.
- Flip-flops and latches map through dfflibmap / the latch techmap as in
  the thin-oxide flow (all 9 flops and 5 latches have liberty + layout);
  the exclude lists mirror the thin-oxide ones (sdfbbp_1, dfrbp_2).
- Tri-states map through SYNTH_TRISTATE_MAP (tribuff_map.v: Yosys $_TBUF_
  onto sg13g2_hv_ebufn_2) with TRISTATE_CELLS listing both footprints,
  mirroring the thin-oxide SCL. No automatic clock gating: an ICG is
  instantiated deliberately, not inferred. To enable it, drop lgcp_1 from
  synth_exclude.cells and set SYNTH_CLOCKGATE_MIN_WIDTH plus
  SYNTH_CLOCKGATE_POSEDGE_ICG "sg13g2_hv_lgcp_1/GATE/CLK/GCLK" -- Yosys
  takes the ICG from that variable, not from the Liberty.
- DFT / scan flows: sdfbbp_map.v puts every posedge flop on the scan cell
  sg13g2_hv_sdfbbp_1. It is opt-in because dfflibmap cannot pattern-match
  a scan-mux next_state. To enable it, remove sg13g2_hv_sdfbbp_1 from
  synth_exclude.cells and set, at DESIGN level (LibreLane does not let a
  PDK set this variable):
    SYNTH_EXTRA_MAPPING_FILE: pdk_dir::libs.tech/librelane/sg13g2_stdcell_hv/sdfbbp_map.v
  All 23 clocked mappings are proven against the Yosys cell semantics with
  equiv_induct; negedge flops are deliberately left unmapped.
- Smoke-tested end to end on an 8-bit counter (ihp-sg13g2-ams-chip-
  template): synthesis, CTS, routing, LVS clean via the auto-derived
  CELL_SPICE_MODELS, no design-level LIB/corner overrides needed.

5. Known limitations:
==========================================================================================
- Not silicon-proven; everything is simulation against the PDK models.
- One Liberty corner only (typ, 3.30 V, 25 C). The characterization flow
  is corner-parameterized (work/corners.py, work/run_corner.sh) and the
  PDK config registers whichever corners are present, so fast
  (3.60 V, -40 C) and slow (3.00 V, 125 C) drop in when characterized.
- No internal_power tables on any cell: the characterization was scoped to
  timing and leakage, so STA power reports see leakage only.
- The min_pulse_width of lgcp_1 and slgcp_1 is identical point for
  point. Their CLK->GCLK delays differ only in the 4th-6th digit (the
  scan leg affects the enable path, not the clock path), which is far
  below the 0.01 ns bisection tolerance, so both land on the same value.
- 11 signal pins sit off the vertical Metal1 track grid and cannot be
  widened within M1.b (12 others were widened onto tracks in the shipped
  GDS). Route with RT_MIN_LAYER Metal1, or expect the router to jog; the
  LEF OBS covers all non-pin Metal1.
- dlygate4sd2_1 and o21ai_1 used gate lengths below the thick-oxide
  minimum; their delay ratios differ from the thin-oxide originals.
- decap_4 / decap_8 store less charge per area (thicker oxide, thin-oxide
  geometry kept).
- Tie cells are a tied-input inv_1 (gate on the diffusion-clamped rail),
  not the four-transistor thin-oxide chain; antenna rule set passes.
- The N-wells satisfy the STRICT analog HV rules (NW.c1/NW.d1 0.62 um),
  not just the DigiBnd digital ones the cells were originally built to:
  work/fix_well_nwc1.py moved the well bottom (2.625 -> 2.570 um, deeper
  jogs over the two deep P-active bands) and widened the lateral
  overhang (0.24 -> 0.62 um). Well-layer-only: zero device changes, no
  re-characterization; Magic reports 0 NW.* errors on the abutted array
  and both KLayout decks stay clean. Residual Magic flags are tool
  interpretation differences on geometry KLayout accepts (Cnt.c contact
  overlap, wide-metal M1.e on PDN rails), plus possible NW.e1 at the
  outermost rows of a block where the VDD tap has only its own 0.24 um
  top overhang (legal under NW.e1.dig; merged away wherever rows abut).
